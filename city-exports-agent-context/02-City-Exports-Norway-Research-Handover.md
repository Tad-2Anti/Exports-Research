4. **Manufacturers and integrated groups can buy external inputs.** Verify the relevant purchasing arrangement; do not reject solely because a business owns a factory.
5. **Onion was not proven to be the sole viable opportunity.** The latest produce evidence reviewed showed competing supply, not a clear shortage.
6. **Kamseen's supposed connection to New Directions Aromatics is unconfirmed.** Similar website wording is not proof of a supplier relationship.
7. **No exact current importer buying price has been verified.** The recorded NOK 249/500 g Sunkost cashew price and NOK 250/10 ml Kamseen lavender price were advertised retail prices at the earlier check. They are not import costs, current quotes or margins.
8. **Repeated stockouts and restocks are useful observations, not sales-volume proof.** Small inventory, corrections, transfers and discontinuations can mislead. Strong businesses can replenish without ever selling out.
9. **Local scarcity or inability to grow a product is not an unfilled order.** Existing imports may already satisfy the market.
10. **No candidate establishes Tamil Nadu supply capability or profitability.** Match the exact product, origin, quality, documentation, capacity and delivered cost before offering it.

## 8. The Simple Category-Expansion Method

Start with a product already in the baseline and one relevant Norwegian account. Run these routes in order, recording sources and removing duplicates:

| Route | Practical action | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Competing distributors | Search the exact product/grade and Norwegian wholesale terms; compare competing catalogues | More companies handling the same category, subject to buying-model verification |
| Brands back to importers | Follow competing brands' labels, official distributor pages and product documents | A possible responsible importer or distributor, not necessarily the original buyer |
| Ingredient users and packers | Find manufacturers whose products require the ingredient, including private-label producers | Additional potential buyers beyond wholesalers |
| Industry networks | Review relevant association members, event exhibitors/speakers and partner lists | Candidate discovery and possible introduction routes |
| Sourcing disclosures | Read company supply-chain reports, supplier requirements and manufacturing information | External purchasing, sourcing regions and assessment requirements where disclosed |
| Published requests | Search EEN for Norway-based buying profiles matching the same product | Stronger intent when a dated sourcing request actually matches |

Helpful Norwegian search terms: **importør** (importer), **grossist** (wholesaler), **råvarer** (raw materials), **leverandør** (supplier), **innkjøp** (purchasing), **produsent** (manufacturer), **kontraktproduksjon** (contract production), **åpenhetsloven** and **aktsomhetsvurdering** (useful terms for supply-chain disclosures).

Sources available for this work include [EEN partnering opportunities](https://een.ec.europa.eu/partnering-opportunities), [Etisk Handel Norge](https://etiskhandel.no/) and its member directory, [DLF](https://www.dlf.no/) for grocery-supplier networks, and [Brønnøysund registers](https://www.brreg.no/en/searching-our-registers/) for legal identity. None substitutes for SKU-specific buying evidence.

Keep two evidence classes:

- **Published or buyer-confirmed sourcing intent:** A dated request that matches the product. Verify whether it remains open.
- **Relevant prospect:** Demonstrable product and purchasing-model fit, but no current requirement confirmed.

Select one narrow initial search, such as ingredient buyers for an existing seed/nut item or outsourced production buyers for an existing textile item. Do not silently broaden to unrelated food, chemicals or clothing just to increase the count.
