# City Exports — Additional Norwegian Buyer Leads in Two Established Categories

## TL;DR
- I identified seven qualified NEW Norwegian prospects (none on the exclude list): for **Category A (dry food ingredients/seeds/nuts)** the strongest are **Brynild Gruppen AS** (Den Lille Nøttefabrikken), **Orkla Foods Norge AS** (Bare Bra granola), **Nøtteblanderen Butikker AS**, and **Idun Industri AS**; for **Category B (souvenir/kitchen textiles and caps)** the strongest are **Nord Suvenir AS**, **Brødrene Flaarønning AS**, and **Norsk Profilreklame & Gaver AS**.
- Almost all leads are **"Relevant prospects"** (demonstrable product + purchasing-model fit) — **none has a dated, published open sourcing request** for these specific products, so buying intent is "unknown/evergreen." Treat none as an open tender.
- India/Tamil Nadu is a **strong** origin for cashews and cotton kitchen textiles/caps, a **genuine but minor-Tamil-Nadu** origin for sesame, and a **poor** fit for cocoa powder (Côte d'Ivoire and Ghana together supplied ~65% of world cocoa in 2024; India is not a listed producer) — so cocoa leads are an origin mismatch unless City Exports re-exports West-African/South-American cocoa.

### India/Tamil Nadu origin plausibility (with mismatches flagged)
- **Strong India fit:** **Cashews** — India accounted for 13.5% of global raw cashew production and 36.5% of global processing share as of 2024 (International Nut and Dried Fruit Council, cited by Mordor Intelligence), and ranked 4th globally in shelled-cashew exports with a 6.60% market share in 2024 (APEDA); Tamil Nadu is among the main cashew-producing states. **Cotton kitchen/tea towels and cotton caps** are also a strong India fit.
- **Genuine India crop but weak Tamil Nadu fit:** **Sesame** — India is the world's 2nd-largest producer (~802,000 t in 2022, FAOSTAT via Helgi Library; India, Sudan and Myanmar together ~44% of 2024 global output per IndexBox), but within India, Gujarat (22.3%) and West Bengal (19.2%) dominate while **Tamil Nadu is only ~4.7%** of national production (FAOSTAT via Tirupati Brokers). Still workable through a Tamil-Nadu-based merchant exporter, but flag the regional-origin nuance to the client.
- **Poor India fit (origin mismatch):** **Cocoa powder** — Côte d'Ivoire (1,850k t) and Ghana (600k t) lead a ~4,840k-tonne world total for cocoa year 2024/25 (ICCO Quarterly Bulletin of Cocoa Statistics, Vol. LI No.1, 28-02-2025), together ~65% of global supply; India is not a listed producer. Cocoa leads (Idun, the Orkla cocoa component) are therefore an origin mismatch for Tamil Nadu unless City Exports re-exports West-African/South-American cocoa. **Chia** is primarily South/Central American; **flaxseed and sunflower** are widely grown but not signature Tamil Nadu crops; **hazelnut**-heavy Norwegian granola recipes are not an India crop at all.

## Recommendations
1. **Prioritise Brynild Gruppen (Category A) first.** It is the best-evidenced fit — an integrated nut packer that already sources some cashews from India. *Stage 1:* obtain the Etisk Handel Norge Redegjørelse 2024 PDF (via the digitalkatalog.no link on their åpenhetsloven page) to confirm current India cashew usage. *Stage 2:* approach nut category management (Hilde Sandmæl) through the official contact form. **Escalate if** the report confirms India is still an active cashew origin.
2. **Nord Suvenir (Category B) is the best textile/cap fit** — it runs a BSCI-audited custom-manufacture program for caps and textiles abroad. *Stage 1:* get the readable åpenhetsloven PDF and a product-development contact. *Stage 2:* pitch BSCI-audited Tamil Nadu cotton caps and printed tea towels. **Escalate if** they will consider onboarding a new-country (India) BSCI factory.
3. **Nøtteblanderen and Orkla Foods Norge (Bare Bra)** are strong secondary Category A targets for sesame/sunflower/pumpkin/flax seed. Confirm the raw-material buyer before outreach.
4. **Brødrene Flaarønning** for printed kitchen/tea towels — confirm they buy these externally and that India (vs their usual "Baltics and the East") is acceptable.
5. **De-prioritise Norsk Profilreklame** unless confirmed to import caps direct; as a small reseller it likely buys through European catalogue houses (e.g., New Wave).
6. **Do NOT treat cocoa-powder leads as India-origin opportunities** unless City Exports can supply Ghana/South-American-origin cocoa; flag this to the client. Benchmark that would change this: City Exports establishing a re-export cocoa line from a West-African/South-American origin.
