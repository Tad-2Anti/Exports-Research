# City Exports Agent Instructions

Read these files before doing City Exports buyer, product or market research:

1. `00-CITY-EXPORTS-AGENT-CONTEXT.md`
2. `04-CITY-EXPORTS-LEAD-RESEARCH-OPERATING-PLAN.md`
3. The relevant source documents in this folder

## Non-negotiable research rules

- City Exports is an Indian merchant exporter, not a manufacturer. Do not invent factories, capacity, certifications, prices, relationships or supplier history.
- Keep the research Norway-first and within the existing product categories unless the user explicitly changes the scope.
- Do not count an organisation as a new lead if it already appears in the source documents or exclusion list.
- Separate three kinds of evidence: import/market proof, buyer/business fit and response proof.
- A product page, retail listing, stockout, market report or customs average does not prove current buying intent, replenishment need, buyer price or an open requirement.
- Use `FACT`, `INFERENCE` and `UNKNOWN` labels wherever the evidence is not direct.
- Prefer official company pages, legal registries, government statistics, procurement pages and dated first-party reports.
- Record exact source URLs and the date checked for every material claim.
- Never construct or guess a personal email address from a naming pattern.
- Find the relevant sourcing/procurement route before drafting outreach. Do not send random catalogue emails.
- Report fewer leads when the evidence is weak. Never fill a quota with low-quality prospects.
- Do not call a prospect a confirmed buyer unless there is a dated request, RFQ, explicit buyer confirmation, sample request or response.
- Do not infer import prices from retail prices.
- Do not silently replace old findings. Add dated updates and preserve the research history.

## Required lead record

Every retained company must include:

- legal identity and website
- location and organisation number where verified
- exact product/category evidence
- buying model
- evidence URLs and dates
- current activity status
- origin/current supplier evidence or `UNKNOWN`
- buying-intent lane
- named procurement contact or official route
- one supportable reason to approach
- unknowns
- one next action
- confidence score

## Output language

Keep explanations simple and direct. Use evidence tables for lead batches. State what is known, what is inferred and what remains unknown. Do not describe catalogue relevance as demand validation.
