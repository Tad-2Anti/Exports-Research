### 2.5 Way Nor AS (P12–P13 — custom souvenir caps, printed kitchen towels)
- Org.nr 984 625 871. 117–122 employees, part of a 14-company group, owned by PE firm **Pegasus Industrier** since 2018. CEO: Atle Korsmo. Revenue ~223M NOK.
- Registered purpose explicitly covers in-house "development, production, and sale" of Nordic-themed goods.
- **Final status: Rejected.** In-house manufacturing/design operation — not a fit for an external raw-material or manufacturing pitch.

### 2.6 Sardines (P14)
- Norwegian retail sardine category (MENY/REMA/SPAR) is dominated by **King Oscar**, owned by **Thai Union Group** (Thailand) since 2014 — a vertically integrated multinational seafood producer with its own canning facility in Gniewino, Poland.
- **Final status: Dead end.** Retail growth is real, but it flows to a company that already sources for itself at global scale — no realistic opening for external supply.

### 2.7 Fresh Produce (P15–P18)
- **BAMA Gruppen AS** confirmed as Norway's dominant fruit/veg importer-distributor (~70% market share, supplies KIWI/MENY/SPAR/REMA), sourcing from 80+ countries with a formal supplier-vetting process (self-assessment + ethics questionnaire).
- BAMA's own reporting shows a strong preference for proximity-based sourcing (heavy reliance on land transport from Spain) to preserve freshness.
- **Cucumber (P15), Tomato (P16), Broccoli (P17): Dead end.** 3–4 week sea transit from India is incompatible with these items' short shelf life — a freight-physics constraint, not a contactability problem.
- **Onion (P18): Genuinely viable.** Long shelf life; India is a major global onion exporter. **This is the one new lead worth pursuing.**
- **Contact:** BAMA Gruppen AS, phone **22 88 05 00**, Postboks 263, Nedre Kallbakkvei 40, 1081 Oslo. Site has a dedicated "Import and suppliers" contact channel, but it is a **web form**, not a listed email — no verified email address found (a third-party data broker's guessed format was explicitly not used, as it's unverified).
- **Status:** Contact info found; outreach not yet drafted.
